### First Caliptra 2.1 Subsystem Silicon Successfully Powers On

The Caliptra Consortium is excited to announce a major milestone for the open
silicon security ecosystem: the first Caliptra 2.1 Subsystem silicon has
successfully powered on, achieving a flawless bring-up with all post-silicon
validation tests passing successfully.

Achieved less than nine months after the release of the Caliptra 2.1 Subsystem,
this milestone highlights both the maturity of the architecture and the strength
of industry collaboration. We are especially grateful to ASPEED Technology Inc.
for leading the integration effort in its next-generation SMC and BMC products
and for helping demonstrate the readiness of the Caliptra 2.1 platform in
silicon.

This achievement builds on the success of Caliptra 1.0, which established the
industry's first open and interoperable hardware root of trust and is now
shipping in production silicon across the ecosystem. With Caliptra 2.0 and 2.1,
the community has expanded that vision into the industry's first fully
open-source, quantum-ready silicon security subsystem, providing a stronger
foundation for secure boot, attestation, recovery, cryptographic services, and
hardware key management.

Beyond the silicon milestone itself, the momentum across the Caliptra ecosystem
continues to accelerate. Companies around the world are actively integrating
Caliptra into new SoCs and infrastructure platforms, contributing to a growing
ecosystem committed to open, interoperable silicon security.

### Caliptra Manufacturing Workshop Brings Together the Ecosystem

That momentum was clearly visible last week at the Caliptra Manufacturing
Workshop in Taipei, Taiwan. The event brought together more than 130
participants from 16 companies, representing a broad cross-section of the
semiconductor and infrastructure industries.

Designed for engineers responsible for silicon, board bring-up, and
manufacturing readiness, the workshop focused on the practical aspects of
provisioning and deploying Caliptra-enabled SoCs at scale. Sessions covered
critical integration topics including:

- Boot and initialization flows
- Debug and validation methodologies
- eFuse provisioning
- Certificate provisioning and lifecycle management
- Manufacturing and provisioning workflows
- Configuration and deployment of Caliptra-based SoCs

The workshop also showcased companies helping to build the broader Caliptra
support ecosystem. For example, PUFsecurity demonstrated how it is building on
Caliptra to provide turnkey integration services and security solutions. These
offerings are complemented by process-specific security technologies such as
entropy and noise-source solutions, physically unclonable function (PUF)
technologies, and eFuse implementations that have been validated for advanced
TSMC process nodes, including 3nm and 2nm technologies.

The strong participation, technical engagement, and growing ecosystem support
underscore a shared industry commitment to advancing open silicon security
standards and accelerating adoption across the market.

We are pleased to share the workshop presentations below, along with photos from
the event. We would like to thank all attendees, contributors, and ecosystem
partners whose collaboration continues to make these milestones possible.

Together, we are building the future of open, interoperable, and quantum-ready
silicon security.
